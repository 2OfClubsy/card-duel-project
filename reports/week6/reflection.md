## What we tried to achieve

During Week 6, the team focused on preparing the product for trial use and transition readiness rather than only adding new functionality.

The main goals were:

- prepare a stable Week 6 trial / handover-candidate release;
- update customer-facing documentation;
- create practical handover guidance;
- prepare public report evidence;
- review transition readiness with the customer;
- collect feedback for Week 7 follow-up work.

The implementation team prepared several visible product improvements:

- the game room was changed and improved;
- the blackjack system was extended with active card usage;
- split functionality was added to the blackjack system;
- work started on betting flow and quota-related mechanics;
- the pause menu was improved with settings and save options;
- additional puzzle-related models were added.

Betting flow and quota-related mechanics were treated as work in progress during Week 6 and should be verified before being described as stable final functionality.
