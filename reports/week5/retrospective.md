# Week 5 Retrospective

## What We Learned

This retrospective content is pending the Sprint Review and team reflection session.

## What Went Well

- The team prepared architecture documentation and ADRs for Sprint 3.
- The Issue-linked PR workflow is intended to remain the central process for the sprint.
- Documentation and UAT planning were prepared as draft artifacts.

## What Should Improve

- Automated verification for 3D gameplay interactions should be strengthened once the implementation is complete.
- Architecture decisions must remain aligned with the actual Godot scene structure.
- Traceability between issues, ADRs, and sprint documentation should be confirmed after the review.

## Next Process Change

The next sprint should include stronger CI checks for architecture documentation and concrete UAT evidence after the customer session.
