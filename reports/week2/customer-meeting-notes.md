# Customer Meeting Notes

These notes are used because the meeting was not recorded.

## Meeting context

The customer review meeting took place on 11 June. The goal of the meeting was to present the current Assignment 2 artifacts, discuss the product direction, and receive customer feedback and approval.

## Participants

* Customer
* Team members
* Moderator
* Note-taker

## Chronological notes

At the beginning of the meeting, the moderator briefly introduced the purpose of the review and explained which artifacts would be presented.

The team presented the current game concept: a horror-inspired card duel game based on risk, opponent interaction, and interesting card mechanics.

The team then presented the documented user stories and explained the selected MoSCoW priorities. The customer reviewed the stories and confirmed that the general product direction was understandable.

After that, the team presented the initial proposed MVP v1 scope. The scope focused on a small playable card duel loop with clear UI, interesting mechanics, and understandable challenges.

The team also demonstrated the Miro prototype, the planned game concept, and the game loop.

The customer approved the documented user stories, MoSCoW priorities, and initial proposed MVP v1 scope.

The customer gave feedback that the user story about a demo version should be changed because a separate demo version is not very relevant for the current course project scope.

The customer also recommended developing the game mostly in 3D because a 2D format would not provide enough immersion and atmosphere for this type of horror-inspired game.

The team discussed this feedback and agreed to update the sixth user story while keeping the same priority. The team also agreed to make the product mostly 3D, while using 2D elements where they are useful, such as UI, cards, menus, and overlays.

## Artifacts presented

* User stories
* MoSCoW priorities
* Initial proposed MVP v1 scope
* Miro prototype
* Game concept
* Game loop

## Customer feedback

The customer gave two main pieces of feedback:

1. The demo version user story should be replaced with a more relevant requirement.
2. The game should be developed mostly in 3D because immersion and horror atmosphere are important for this product.

## Decisions and approvals

The customer approved:

* Documented user stories
* MoSCoW priorities
* Initial proposed MVP v1 scope

The team decided to:

* Replace the old US-06 about the demo version.
* Add a new US-06 about immersive 3D presentation.
* Keep the priority of US-06 as Should Have.
* Continue the product direction as a mostly 3D game with some 2D UI/card elements.

## Resulting changes

The previous US-06 was replaced with a new story focused on immersive 3D presentation.

The product direction was updated: the game will be mostly 3D, but 2D elements may still be used for interface, cards, menus, and overlays.
