## Sprint Review summary

During the meeting, the team showed the customer the current Sprint Backlog and the current MVP v2 direction.

The customer generally approved the Sprint Backlog and confirmed that the planned work direction is understandable.

The team also showed the progress made since MVP v1. During this week, the team added:

- pause menu;
- ability to select cards from the table;
- foundation for a blackjack system.

The blackjack system is not fully playable yet. The customer noted that it should not be presented as a fully completed MVP v2 feature until the full blackjack gameplay works correctly.

## Addressed previous feedback

The customer confirmed that the project continues to follow one of the main early feedback points: the game should be developed as a 3D experience rather than a simple 2D prototype.

The customer liked the current game style and approved that the team continues to keep the game in a 3D direction.
