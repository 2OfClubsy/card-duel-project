## Sprint Review result

The team showed the customer the current Sprint Backlog and the current MVP v2 direction.

The customer generally approved the Sprint Backlog and confirmed that the work direction is acceptable.

Compared to MVP v1, the team showed the following progress:

- pause menu;
- ability to select cards from the table;
- foundation for a blackjack system.

The customer approved MVP v2 in general, but noted that the blackjack system should be completed before it is presented as a fully working gameplay feature.
